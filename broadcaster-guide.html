Home Player Guide

⚙️ GETTING STARTED

1. Open the game and click SETTINGS in the main menu.

2. Under TARGET CHANNEL click on CONNECT and login with your Twitch Broadcaster account.

3. (Optional) If you have a custom bot account for Twitch, under BOT ACCOUNT click CONNECT and enter your bot Twitch credentials.

4. Once connected, click SAVE & CLOSE at the bottom of the GAME CONFIGURATION menu.

5. From the main menu, select SOLO RACE, TEAM RACE, or RELAY RACE to open a new lobby and players can immediately begin typing !join in your chat to enter the lobby!

6. Game toggles such as PARTY, ELIM and GAUNTLET can be toggled on or off from both the main menu, and the active race lobby screen.

7. START RACING! And enjoy!
