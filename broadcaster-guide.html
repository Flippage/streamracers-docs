⚙️ GETTING STARTED

1. Open the game and click SETTINGS in the main menu.

2. Enter your Twitch Username and hit Save.

3. Click the red OFFLINE pill in the top right corner of the main menu to connect to your Twitch Chat.

Once connected, it will turn green, and players can immediately begin typing !join in your chat to enter the lobby!
